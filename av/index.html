The Sadhnas

How to do each one 🌄
Before you begin, sit straight, close your eyes and visualise the bright white light of Tirthankar Paramatma. Practised with faith and devotion, sadhana becomes aradhana — and what you truly believe in, happens.

Which one, when?

🧠 When your brain isn't working
Arihantanam
💪 When your confidence is low
Siddhanam
🔋 When you feel weak or tired
Aayriyanam

Focus on the mantra — and switch on the power of the sadhana. ☀️
